# Bodhuno competitor landscape — 15 November 2026

## Executive finding

**First direct competitor to monitor: TS Police Hub (Anvil Edtech).** It targets the same narrow audience as Bodhuno—Telangana Police SI and Constable candidates—and combines English/Telugu practice, CBT-style mocks, PYQs, notes, current affairs and learner analytics. Its website and Google Play listing describe a broader product than Bodhuno's current pilot.

**Second direct competitor / content benchmark: Brolly Exam Prep.** It is a Telangana Police-focused web and Android practice product with a large published test catalogue (176 tests on its exam page), including previous papers, full mocks and chapter tests. Its page says 175 of 176 tests are free.

Testbook and Adda247 are larger national exam-preparation platforms with Telangana Police offerings. They are important distribution and pricing benchmarks, but less focused than TS Police Hub and Brolly.

This is a public-web snapshot, not a verified market-share ranking. Competitor feature counts and prices can change; recheck before using them in marketing.

## What Bodhuno has today

Based on the repository README on 15 Nov 2026:

- Mobile-first, bilingual English/Telugu and guest-first; no mandatory registration.
- Police SI: 127 questions across six short collections, plus a separate 200-question full-mock preview.
- Police Constable: 128 questions across six short collections, plus a separate 200-question full-mock preview.
- Local progress dashboard, topic-wise practice, wrong-answer revision, saved questions, streaks and personalised daily plan.
- Static JSON catalogue and browser-local storage; no database or paid AI dependency.
- Sets 02–04 and the full-mock previews are described as awaiting independent subject-expert and Telugu review. The README explicitly says current-affairs coverage is a dated November 2026 preview, not comprehensive or automatically updated.

## Competitive gap and opportunity

Bodhuno already has meaningful learner-retention features: saved questions, wrong-answer revision, topic analytics, streaks and a personalised daily plan. Those are not enough on their own—TS Police Hub also advertises topic analytics, and Brolly / PRACBOOK advertise revision-oriented features.

The biggest gap is **depth and trust of the question bank**:
1. Competitors publicly claim much larger test libraries and/or PYQ collections.
2. Bodhuno's short collections are limited, and its 200-question mock previews still need independent academic and Telugu review.
3. The current-affairs preview is dated and not yet a dependable daily habit.
4. Bodhuno has no cross-device account sync or public ranking; that is acceptable for a low-cost pilot, but should be a deliberate product choice.

## Recommended Bodhuno strategy

Do not copy a competitor's feature checklist. Build a narrower promise and execute it reliably:

1. **Accuracy before volume.** Publish only questions with verified answer keys, official/source references, clear explanations and reviewed Telugu. Keep unreviewed content labelled preview.
2. **A dependable daily 10-question routine.** Continue the existing personalised daily plan, but make the daily set genuinely fresh, syllabus-mapped and quality checked. Avoid recycling the same questions.
3. **Telangana-specific advantage.** Make Telangana history, movement, geography, economy, polity, culture and state current affairs a visible strength, with source dates and revision.
4. **Transparent mock readiness.** Keep short practice distinct from full-length simulations. Ensure the full mock matches the current official pattern and marking rules before publication.
5. **Stay guest-first and fast.** No forced sign-up, no ads during tests, low-data mobile experience and local progress. Consider optional sync only after users ask for it.
6. **Build trust visibly.** Show question source, last-verified date, explanation, Telugu review status and corrections process. Never imply affiliation with TGPRB.

## First 30-day priorities

- **Week 1:** Audit every published/preview SI and Constable item for factual correctness, duplicates, syllabus mapping, answer rationale and Telugu quality. Freeze publication of anything unverified.
- **Week 2:** Prepare and independently review a fresh SI + Constable daily-practice batch from official sources; add question-level source/date metadata where missing.
- **Week 3:** Review the 200-question mock structure and scoring against the current official notification; publish only after independent review.
- **Week 4:** Run mobile usability and learner feedback checks; measure practice starts, completion, return visits and wrong-answer revision usage without collecting unnecessary personal data.
