# Bodhuno competitor research — Telangana Police SI & Constable

**Research date:** 15 November 2026  
**Scope:** Publicly visible products serving Telangana Police SI / Constable aspirants. Download counts and feature descriptions below are third-party/store-published claims, not independently audited active-user figures.

## Executive summary

Bodhuno is entering an established market. The strongest direct install-scale benchmark found in this scan is **TG Police Constable & SI Prep** by HSN Software Technologies, which lists **50K+ Google Play downloads**. A newer, tightly positioned product, **TS Police Hub** by Anvil Edtech, is a useful feature benchmark: it lists 1K+ downloads and describes bilingual CBT mocks, PYQs, notes, daily current affairs, topic analytics, rankings and physical-training discovery.

**Recommendation:** Do not try to win by claiming the largest question count or by copying every feature. Build trust through accurate, source-backed Telangana questions, a genuinely useful free daily habit, transparent exam-pattern scoring, and fast weak-topic revision.

## Competitor 1 — TG Police Constable & SI Prep (HSN Software Technologies)

- Google Play listing: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hsn.tgpoliceexams
- Store listing shows **50K+ downloads** (observed 15 Nov 2026).
- Focus: Telangana Police Constable and SI.
- Advertised features: model papers, timed full mocks, previous-year papers, subject-wise tests, detailed explanations and score/performance review.
- Subject coverage named in the listing: arithmetic, reasoning, Telangana history/culture/movement, geography, economy, GK/current affairs, polity, science and English.
- The listing says it was updated 4 Dec 2025; this is the listing's displayed update date, not evidence of current maintenance.
- Competitive implication: generic mock/PYQ/subject practice is not enough differentiation for Bodhuno.

## Competitor 2 — TS Police Hub (Anvil Edtech)

- Google Play listing: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.anviledtech.tspolicehub
- Store listing shows **1K+ downloads** (observed 15 Nov 2026); updated 30 Oct 2026.
- Focus: Telangana SI and Constable, explicitly bilingual English/Telugu.
- Advertised features: CBT-style timed/untimed full mocks and sectionals, instant scoring with negative marking, rank/percentile, solved bilingual PYQs, subject notes, daily current-affairs digest, topic analytics, free daily quiz, PET grounds and coach directory.
- Its latest-notes text advertises a free Sunday 200-question Grand Test.
- Competitive implication: this is a close product/feature benchmark. Bodhuno should not assume that bilingual UI, daily quizzes, weak-area analytics or mock tests are unique.

## Competitor 3 — Testbook (large multi-exam platform)

- Telangana SI page: https://testbook.com/prep/study-material/telangana-police-si/
- Telangana Constable page: https://testbook.com/prep/study-material/telangana-police-constable/
- SI page currently lists 223 mock tests, 9 previous-year papers and 20.3K students enrolled in its test series.
- Constable page currently lists 251 mock tests and 7 previous-year papers.
- Advertises Telugu live classes, free live classes/quizzes, All-India rank, PDF notes and a ₹1 two-day trial.
- Competitive implication: Testbook competes on breadth, content volume, faculty and distribution. Bodhuno should not attempt to match its catalogue breadth immediately.

## What Bodhuno currently has (repository README and question catalogue)

- Mobile-first, bilingual English/Telugu, guest-first experience.
- Local progress dashboard, topic practice, weak-area identification, wrong-answer revision, saved questions, streak calendar and personalised daily study plan.
- Static JSON question bank and catalogue; no account, database or paid AI dependency in the current pilot.
- README currently documents 127 SI and 128 Constable questions across short practice collections, plus 200-question full-mock previews.
- Only the foundation collections are marked published; additional collections and full mocks remain previews pending independent subject and Telugu review.
- README explicitly states coverage is incomplete and current-affairs preview is dated, not comprehensive.

## Competitive gap and recommended response

1. **Close the content trust gap first.** Expand syllabus coverage systematically. Every current-affairs item should show its publication date and primary official source. Preserve the distinction between official PYQs and original practice questions.
2. **Protect quality over raw volume.** Add a repeatable reviewer checklist for answer-key verification, source verification, syllabus mapping, difficulty and Telugu translation. Do not publish unreviewed AI drafts.
3. **Make the daily loop the product.** Put today's short practice, continue learning, weak-topic revision and a weekly mock in a simple home screen. Make the next best action obvious without forcing sign-in.
4. **Show measurable learning.** Keep the existing progress, streak, saved-question and wrong-answer tools; make the result screen clearly tell the learner what to revise next.
5. **Build trust and conversion ethically.** Keep core learning free as documented. If monetisation is tested later, use a clearly labelled optional ad-free pass; never put ads inside an active test or sell a better rank.
6. **Validate with real aspirants.** Run 10–15 moderated usability sessions with Telangana SI/Constable candidates before adding expensive features such as live classes, chat or AI tutoring.

## Suggested next product milestone

**Bodhuno Daily Preparation Loop v1**
- A clear “Today's 10 questions” entry point for SI and Constable.
- A source/date label for current-affairs sets.
- A weekly full-mock schedule and a post-test revision path.
- A simple content coverage indicator showing published vs preview material.
- A short feedback action for reporting a wrong answer or translation.

Do not claim competitor feature gaps that cannot be verified from public listings. Recheck app listings and pricing before making any public comparison or purchasing decision.
